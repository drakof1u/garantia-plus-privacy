Privacy Policy

Image to PDF NoWatermark respects your privacy.

1. Data Collection

This application does not collect, store, or share personal data.

All image to PDF conversions are processed locally on your device.

2. Offline Processing

The app works 100% offline. Your images and PDF files are never uploaded to any server.

3. Advertisements

This app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect certain anonymous data in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

Learn more here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time PRO purchase to remove ads and unlock advanced features.

All payments are securely processed by Google Play. The developer does not collect payment information.

5. Data Security

Since the app does not collect or store personal data, no user data is transmitted or stored externally.
